Device: Sony WH-1000XM5
Reported Issue: Broken headband hinge (customer provided the replacement hinge).

Carefully routed the bare wiring harness through the new structural hinge assembly, and then meticulously re-pinned the micro-connector in the exact original sequence. Reassembled the headset and verified full sensor, audio, and active noise cancellation (ANC) functionality.
