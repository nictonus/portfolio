Ticket ID: IT-14256
Device: Lenovo Legion 5 Pro 16ARH7H
Reported Issue: Preventative thermal maintenance to resolve fan noise and thermal throttling.
----------------------------------------
Performed Work: Precision thermal restoration. Lenovo Legion laptops require specific thermal interfaces to maintain factory cooling efficiency. Carefully removed the old, hardened thermal compound. Treated the bare CPU/GPU silicon and heatsink surfaces with electronics-grade solvents. Applied factory-spec Phase-Change Material (PCM, Honeywell PTM7950 equivalent) to strictly adhere to OEM thermal design parameters. Reassembled and stress-tested to confirm sustained peak performance without thermal capping.
