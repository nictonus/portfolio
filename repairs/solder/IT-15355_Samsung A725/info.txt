Ticket ID: IT-15355
Device: Samsung Galaxy A72 (SM-A725)
Reported Issue: Initially experiencing screen freezing[cite: 11]. A previous third-party repair shop attempted a display replacement, resulting in a completely dead device, which they misdiagnosed as a fatal motherboard failure.
----------------------------------------
Performed Work: Conducted deep hardware diagnostics using a laboratory power supply. Microscopic logic board inspection revealed severe oxidation (corrosion) and physically torn contact pads on the main FPC connector, directly caused by the previous technician's mishandling. Performed precision micro-soldering to remove the damaged connector, rebuilt the torn PCB copper pads, and installed a brand-new FPC connector. Fully restored the motherboard's functionality and display communication, proving the previous "dead motherboard" diagnosis wrong.
