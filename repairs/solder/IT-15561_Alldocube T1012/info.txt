Ticket ID: IT-15561
Device: Alldocube T1012
Reported Issue: Device not charging due to physical damage.
----------------------------------------
Performed Work: Component-level replacement of the Type-C system connector. Microscopic inspection revealed torn PCB pads and broken logic board traces caused by mechanical stress. Performed precision micro-soldering to rebuild the damaged copper traces. Soldered a new Type-C port and applied UV-curable solder mask to insulate the repaired tracks and structurally reinforce the connector's mounting points. Successfully restored stable power delivery.
