Ticket ID: IT-14955
Device: Samsung SM-A336B/DSN
Reported Issue: Device dead/not powering on after severe physical impact, suspected display, battery, or flex cable failure.
----------------------------------------
Diagnostic Report: Advanced logic board diagnostics to isolate the exact point of failure. Secured the motherboard on a thermal preheating station (Sunshine LS4) for detailed PCB inspection. Successfully identified a severely damaged inter-board FPC connector as the root cause of the power and communication loss.
Outcome: Compiled a detailed fault report and quoted the client for a precision SMD connector replacement. The repair was declined by the customer due to cost (BER - Beyond Economic Repair for the client). Device was safely reassembled and returned.
*Note: Showcases accurate component-level fault finding and professional PCB thermal preparation.*
