Ticket ID: IT-15082
Device: Samsung Galaxy A34 (SM-A346E)
Reported Issue: Device dead / Suspected battery failure.
----------------------------------------
Performed Work: Advanced diagnostics revealed that the internal battery pack had detached from the chassis adhesive. The resulting mechanical movement inside the housing applied severe stress to the motherboard, ultimately ripping the battery FPC connector off its solder pads. Performed precision micro-soldering to remove the damaged connector and successfully soldered a new FPC connector to the logic board. Installed a new replacement battery pack and fully restored the thermal interface and structural adhesive to permanently secure the cell and prevent future mechanical sheer stress.
