Micro-Soldering & SMD Repair

Component-level diagnostics, trace restoration, SMD component replacement, and connector repairs.

Case 01: Type-C Port Replacement & Damaged Pads Restoration

Issue: Loose USB Type-C port, device not charging, data transfer failing.

Work Done: Safe desoldering of broken mechanical housing, cleaning torn data pads from the PCB, running micro-jumpers, and installing a reinforced OEM-grade connector.

Case 02: Power Management SMD Chip Replacement

Issue: Laptop turns on for 2 seconds and shuts down, short on secondary rail.

Work Done: Localized defective charging controller using low-voltage injection, removed shorted SMD chip with hot air station, cleared flux residues, and mounted a fresh component.
